FEX correspondence:
T models CallHostFunction<signature> code captured by native-PFN CustomIR H -> T.
U models CallbackUnpack<signature>::Unpack stored as GuestUnpacker in a host-owned TrampolineInstanceInfo.
H models a native PFN whose host/native library lifetime is longer than the guest wrapper.
G models an application or other guest-DSO callback target whose lifetime is independent of the thunk wrapper.

Conclusion boundary:
Moving T and U into a process-resident bridge image is sufficient in this loader model to remove the guest-wrapper unload dependency from both FEX-created bridge directions while preserving true wrapper unload/reset semantics. This does not prove FEX generator integration, resolve raw-H alias/provenance ambiguity, or make an independently unloaded G safe.
