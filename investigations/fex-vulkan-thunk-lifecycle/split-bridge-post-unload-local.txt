Split resident bridge: post-wrapper-unload native-target call

Local architecture: x86_64
Result:
SPLIT_BRIDGE_PASS adapter=<ASLR> post_unload_call=42 bridge_maps=5 native_maps=5 wrapper_resets=1 bridge_calls=3

Sequence proved:
- native target DSO stays loaded and supplies H;
- bridge DSO has DF_1_NODELETE and supplies stable adapter T;
- ordinary wrapper DSO loads and has resettable static generation state;
- T(H,35) == 42 while wrapper is loaded;
- wrapper dlclose removes all wrapper mappings;
- T(H,35) == 42 after wrapper mappings are gone;
- wrapper reopen resets static state and returns same T;
- 1000 wrapper close/reopen cycles end with wrapper unmapped while bridge/native mapping counts stay unchanged.

Relation to hosted evidence:
The earlier split-runtime workflow passed on hosted x86-64 and AArch64 but only called the adapter while each wrapper generation was mapped. This local variant adds the missing FEX-like case: the native H remains live and the resident T is invoked after the guest-wrapper analogue has physically unloaded.

Evidence boundary:
This is loader/lifetime design evidence, not a FEX integration run. It does not prove the FEX generator can yet emit its CallHostFunction/special-thunk pair into a resident sidecar, nor that the real Vulkan exit-139 arrives through the dynamic-PFN CustomIR route.
