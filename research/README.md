# Research Rounds

This directory holds dated landscape surveys and the source orientation behind them. Durable programmes and formal lane directories live in [`../programmes/`](../programmes/).

## In simple words

A research round surveys broad territory. It can add possibilities to the programme registry, promote strong possibilities into formal lane directories, and identify recurring upstream targets. It does not carry active evidence forever in one giant file.

## Layout

```text
research/
├── README.md
└── rounds/
    └── <date>-<round>/
        ├── README.md
        ├── selection.md
        └── sources.md
```

## Promotion path

1. A round identifies a possible lane.
2. `programmes/registry.yml` records it at `inbox`.
3. A programme `STATUS.md` holds durable direction.
4. A formal lane directory appears when the bounded question and first probe are clear.
5. An investigation appears when source reading and execution begin against an exact revision.
6. General lessons move into `notes/`; repeatable evidence stays in `investigations/`.

## Research-round discipline

Keep surveys compact once their outputs have been normalized into programmes, lanes, targets, notes, and investigations. Preserve selection reasoning and sources inside the dated round directory.
